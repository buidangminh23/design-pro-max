# Tally

Tally is a macOS menu-bar app that tracks time per project with one click.

Who reads the page: freelancers who bill by the hour.
Primary action: download the free version.

Features:
- Start and stop a timer from the menu bar without opening a window.
- Weekly totals per project, exported as CSV for invoices.
- Idle detection that asks before keeping time you were away.

Pricing:
- Free: one project, CSV export.
- Pro: 4 USD per month, unlimited projects and idle detection.

FAQ:
- Does Tally sync between Macs? Not yet; data stays on the Mac.
- Can I import from another tracker? Yes, from CSV.
- Is there a Windows version? No.
