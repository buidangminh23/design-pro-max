# Search panel

What the panel looks like in the app, for the rebuild:

- A rounded panel 640 px wide with a search field on top: a magnifier icon, the placeholder "Search notes" and a shortcut hint on the right.
- Below it, a results list of up to five rows. Each row has a coloured dot for the notebook, the note title in bold and a one-line excerpt with the matched words highlighted.
- The selected row has a soft accent background.

The demo: the user types "quarterly plan", results narrow as each word is typed, the second result gets selected and its row highlights. Our notes and titles are fictional sample data.

The clip sits in a card 640 CSS px wide at 16:9 on our landing page, below the fold. The page is light.
