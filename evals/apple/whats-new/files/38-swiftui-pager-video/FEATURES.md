# Ledger 3.0 (macOS 14 and iOS 17 and later)

Three features, in this order:

1. Badge: New. Title: Sync across devices. Body: Your books stay up to date on every Mac and iPhone signed in to the same account. Media: Resources/sync.mov, an 8 second screen clip with no audio (not in this folder).
2. Badge: Faster. Title: Instant search. Body: Results appear as you type, across every book and every year. Media: Resources/search.heic.
3. Badge: Redesigned. Title: A calmer sidebar. Body: Books, tags and reports now share one sidebar you can collapse. Media: none.
